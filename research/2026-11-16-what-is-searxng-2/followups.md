## Who maintains SearXNG

- SearXNG is **driven by an open community** and is maintained by the **SearXNG team** (its codebase is released under AGPL-3.0 and hosted under the `searxng` GitHub organization / `searxng/searxng` repository) [1][3].
- It is a **fork of searX**, created by a **former searX maintainer**; the fork happened because the majority of searX maintainers did not consider the newly proposed features (notably engine metrics) privacy-respecting enough [4]. The project is now commonly referenced by the "SearXNG team," with development tracked on its GitHub repository, translations handled via Weblate (Codeberg), and community discussion on the `#searxng:matrix.org` Matrix channel [1][3].

Note: the saved sources describe the maintainers collectively (the SearXNG team / open community) rather than naming specific individual maintainers, so no specific names can be cited here [1][3].
